ORBIT
Convert, resize, compress and crop images, and merge, split and compress PDFs.

Everything happens on your computer. Your files are never uploaded, and there's
no account to create.


INSTALL (Windows)

1. Double-click Orbit_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe. You don't need admin rights.
2. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
   This appears because the app isn't code-signed.
3. Open Orbit from the shortcut on your Desktop or from the Start menu.

Tested on Windows 11. On Windows 10, setup may need internet to download a
component called WebView2. There's no Mac version.

If Windows blocks the installer with no "Run anyway" option, Smart App Control
is probably on. Trying again a little later sometimes works.


HOW TO USE IT

1. Drag files onto the Orbit window.
2. Pick an action from the wheel.
3. Choose your settings and start.

Your original files are never changed. New files are saved next to the originals
unless you choose another folder, and nothing is ever overwritten.


WHAT IT CAN DO

Images (PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP, TIFF)
  Convert    Change the format
  Resize     By percent, or to fit a width and height
  Compress   Make the file smaller
  Crop       One image at a time
  To PDF     Make one PDF from several images

PDFs
  Merge      Join 2 or more PDFs into one
  Split      Save every page, or the pages you choose, as separate PDFs
  Compress   Make the file smaller by shrinking its images. Text stays sharp

Audio and video files are recognised, but they can't be processed yet.


GOOD TO KNOW

- Compress saves nothing if the result wouldn't be smaller, and tells you why.
- Animated GIFs keep only their first frame when converted, resized, cropped or
  added to a PDF. Compress skips GIFs.
- Merging PDFs doesn't keep bookmarks or form fields.
- Password-protected and digitally signed PDFs are left alone.


SHIFT + DRAG

While Orbit is open, hold Shift and drag files from File Explorer or the desktop.
Orbit comes to your cursor so you can drop them on it.

Drop them on Orbit. Letting go over Explorer instead may move the files, because
that's what Shift + drag normally does in Windows. Press Esc to cancel.

To make this work, Orbit checks whether Shift and the left mouse button are held,
and where the cursor is, while it's open. It doesn't read other keys or record
anything.


UNINSTALL

Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Orbit > Uninstall.
